Method

What makes a useful research question.

I start with a failure pattern that cannot be explained well by existing operational checks. The next step is to separate the local incident from the general mechanism: what conditions make the failure possible, what evidence would reveal it, and what intervention changes the outcome?

The aim is not to turn every production lesson into a grand theory. It is to make the reasoning clear enough that someone else can challenge it, reproduce it, or apply it in a different system.
