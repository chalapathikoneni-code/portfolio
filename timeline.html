Timeline

A career shaped by the same question in different systems.

The path from transaction platforms to trustworthy AI was not a sudden change of field. Each stage exposed a deeper version of the problem of reliable evidence.

2006–10

Transaction and inventory systems

Early work in retail data and operational integration. Learned that the meaning of inventory depends on timing, state transitions, and reconciliation across systems.

2010–12

Enterprise retail and supply chain

Expanded into large-scale retail data, forecasting inputs, and cross-domain integration.

2012–16

Finance and supply-chain models

Worked on analytical systems where business definitions, governance, and repeatability mattered as much as computation.

2016–21

Forecasting, replenishment, and platform modernization

Engineering around commercial forecasting systems, multi-brand integration, simulation, controls, and early cloud data modernization.

2021–now

Measurement, data reliability, and applied AI

Enterprise-scale data systems supporting analytics and science, followed by research into silent corruption, observability, reconciliation, and trustworthy AI.

2025–now

Research and professional service

Publications, conference presentations, reviewing, TPC leadership, and work that turns recurring production failures into testable research questions.
