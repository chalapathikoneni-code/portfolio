## Research question

How should incident analysis account for missing, delayed, or contradictory telemetry?

## Core idea

Treat telemetry quality as a first-class signal. Incident response should reason about both system behavior and the reliability of the evidence used to infer that behavior.

## Why it matters

Without evidence-quality controls, a response system may produce confident explanations from incomplete observations.

## Status

Accepted public research work on distributed stream processing and evidence integrity.
