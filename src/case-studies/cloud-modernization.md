Moving data to a new platform is the visible part of modernization. Preserving definitions, controls, ownership, and confidence is the harder part.

## What made it hard

Legacy platforms accumulate years of business assumptions. Some are documented. Many live in schedules, transformations, exception handling, and operational habits. A direct technical translation can preserve rows while changing meaning.

## How I approached it

I focused on migration patterns that paired data movement with reconciliation, validation, lineage, and governed access. The aim was not only to reproduce outputs, but to make differences visible and explainable.

## Lesson I still use

A migration is complete only when users can trust the new answer for the same reasons they trusted—or challenged—the old one.
