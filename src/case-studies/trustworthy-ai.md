A strong model cannot compensate for stale context, ambiguous definitions, missing lineage, or unreliable feedback.

## What made it hard

Generative systems can create outputs that are structurally valid and semantically wrong. Traditional checks may accept them because the format looks correct.

## How I approach it

I treat the model as one component in a controlled data path. Ownership, provenance, reconciliation, evidence quality, and safe fallback behavior are part of the system design.

## Lesson I still use

AI does not create trust. It inherits the strengths and weaknesses of the systems around it.
