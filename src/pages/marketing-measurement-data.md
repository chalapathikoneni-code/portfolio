It does not describe confidential employer systems, internal programs, clients, agencies, architectures, metrics, or operational practices.</p><h2>Why measurement data is difficult</h2><p>Measurement systems combine changing taxonomies, delayed signals, multiple platforms, attribution assumptions, and data that may be individually valid but collectively inconsistent.</p><h2>Safe, reusable engineering principles</h2><ul><li>Define metrics and dimensions consistently before scaling pipelines.</li><li>Preserve lineage from source evidence to reported outcome.</li><li>Detect late, missing, duplicated, and semantically incompatible data.</li><li>Separate measurement methodology from implementation-specific details.</li><li>Make recalculation and correction reproducible.</li></ul><p>This perspective connects to <a href="/data-quality-engineering.html">data quality engineering</a> and <a href="/distributed-data-systems.html">distributed-system reliability</a>.</p></article></div></section>
