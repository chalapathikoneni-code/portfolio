<header class="page-hero topic-hero"><div class="wrap"><div class="eyebrow">AI for Data Engineering</div><h1>Use AI to strengthen engineering judgment—not to hide uncertainty.</h1><p>AI is most useful in data engineering when its recommendations remain bounded by deterministic controls and traceable evidence.</p></div></header><section><div class="wrap article-body"><aside class="article-aside"><strong>Chalapathi Koneni</strong><span>Senior Data Engineering Leader</span><a href="/engineering.html">Engineering work →</a><a href="/research.html">Research →</a></aside><article class="prose topic-prose"><h2>Where AI can help</h2><p>AI can assist with semantic validation, anomaly explanation, incident triage, log interpretation, and generation of candidate remediation steps.</p><h2>Design principles</h2><ul><li>Keep authoritative checks outside the model.</li><li>Expose confidence and supporting evidence.</li><li>Evaluate on realistic failure modes, including silent corruption.</li><li>Prevent generated recommendations from executing without appropriate controls.</li><li>Measure whether AI improves diagnosis, not merely whether it produces fluent text.</li></ul><p>See <a href="/case-studies/trustworthy-ai.html">trustworthy AI engineering</a> and <a href="/research.html">related research</a>.</p></article></div></section>
