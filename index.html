Enterprise data systems · Applied research

Designing data systems people can trust.

I have spent more than two decades working on the engineering behind forecasting, measurement, analytics, and AI-assisted decision making. The recurring question is simple: when data is incomplete, inconsistent, or changing, how do we know the decision built on it is still sound?

The thread

One problem, many forms.

My work has moved through retail forecasting, supply-chain data, cloud platforms, measurement systems, and applied AI. The technology changed. The underlying problem did not.

Important decisions often depend on several systems agreeing with one another. Product data, inventory, pricing, promotions, finance, and analytical models can each be technically correct and still describe different versions of the business.

Much of my work has been about finding those disagreements early, making them explainable, and designing systems that recover without hiding uncertainty.
